A personal portfolio / landing page for **Cindy**, a French *graphiste éco-responsable* (eco-responsible graphic designer). The brand reads as: warm, editorial, French, slightly literary, anti-corporate, anti-flashy, eco-aligned. Think small-batch press / artisanal magazine rather than tech-startup landing page.

## Content fundamentals

### Language
**French only** (`fr`). All UI copy, all body copy, all CTAs.

### Address form
**Vouvoiement.** Cindy addresses the reader as *vous*, never *tu*. This is consistent with mission-driven small-business clients in France — formal but warm.

### Sentence-level voice
- **First person singular** when speaking as Cindy in the about section: *"je conseille", "Faire son site"*.
- **Third person** in section headers framing the brand: *"Cindy, graphiste éco-responsable à l'écoute de vos valeurs"*.
- **Direct address** in the hero and offer copy: *"votre communication", "vos valeurs"*.

### Casing
- **Sentence case** for headlines (`Votre communication ne devrait pas devenir un frein à votre activité.`). Never Title Case.
- **lowercase** for tertiary descriptors and value props (`beau, mais surtout utilisable`, `cohérents dans le temps`, `conçus pour inspirer confiance`).
- **UPPERCASE + tracked** for tiny section labels (`CÔTÉ STRUCTURES`, `CÔTÉ TOOLS`, `CONCLUSION`) and small navigation.
- **Title cased** is avoided.

### Italics
Heavy editorial use of italics for emphasis on a single word inside a serif headline — e.g. *"Cindy, graphiste **éco-responsable** à l'écoute de vos valeurs"*. The italic is the rhetorical hand-on-heart.

### Tone keywords
warm · editorial · sober · grounded · hand-felt · honest · slightly literary · zero hype

### What we never do
- ❌ No emoji. Period.
- ❌ No exclamation points (except very sparingly — once per page max).
- ❌ No tech-y verbs ("disrupt", "scale", "transform", "unlock"). Cindy *conseille*, *écoute*, *accompagne*.
- ❌ No anglicisms when a French word exists. ("brief" → "cahier"; "branding" → "identité"; "design system" stays, grudgingly.)
- ❌ No data-slop stats ("3× faster", "+47% conversion") — totally off-brand.

> Section sub: *"Quelques projets choisis"*

## Visual foundations

### Palette — fixed by user (2026-05-23)

| Role | Name | Hex | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary surface 1 | **Terre** | `#412F21` | warm chocolate-earth |
| Primary surface 2 | **Nuit**  | `#051833` | deep ink navy |
| Secondary accent (pairs with Terre) | **Glycine** | `#E4D1FE` | soft lavender |
| Secondary accent (pairs with Nuit)  | **Brume**   | `#BAD1FD` | misted light blue |
| Type on Terre | **Paille** | `#F2E5C2` | warm parchment yellow — ⚠️ flagged, awaiting user confirmation |
| Type on Nuit  | **Brume**  | `#BAD1FD` | reused as type |
| Default canvas (bone) | `#fbf6e8` | a very pale Paille tint, when no primary is appropriate |

The legacy cocoa / cream / ink-blue / sage / coral system was retired in this revision.

### Pairing rules

- **Terre surface → Paille type → Glycine italic emphasis.** Inside running prose, italic words are tinted Glycine (or Glycine-900 on bone).
- **Nuit surface → Brume type → Brume italic emphasis** (slightly opaque-shifted; we don't introduce a second accent on Nuit).
- **Bone canvas → Terre type → Glycine-900 italic emphasis.**
- Glycine and Brume **never share a section.** Each is the partner of exactly one primary.
- Solid-color cards on Terre are **Paille**; solid-color cards on Nuit are **Brume**; cards on bone are **parchment** (`#fffaef`).

### Section rhythm of a page

```
[Terre header]  →  [Terre hero]  →  [Bone projects]  →  [Nuit manifesto]  →  [Terre supports]  →  [Bone about]  →  [Nuit footer]
```

The rhythm Terre / Bone / Nuit / Terre / Bone / Nuit is the brand's signature. Resist using only one primary throughout a page — the alternation is the point.

### Typography
- **Display**: Cormorant Garamond (substitute — see flag in `colors_and_type.css`), 300–400 weight. Italics are part of the everyday register, not a special case, and are colour-tinted (Glycine or Glycine-900).
- **Body**: EB Garamond at 17px with generous line-height (1.55–1.65). Serif body is non-negotiable for the brand.
- **Micro / nav / button labels**: Inter Tight, 11–12px, UPPERCASE, tracked at 0.10–0.16em.

Headlines almost always break across two lines with `text-wrap: balance` and an italic word for emphasis.

### Spacing & rhythm
Section padding is generous — `110–128px` top/bottom for major sections, `48–64px` for sub-sections. Card padding is `22–32px`. The page feels airy and paper-like, never compressed.

### Backgrounds
- Mostly flat color fills. No gradients of the bluish-purple variety.
- Hero imagery is **warm photography** — laptops on linen, hands holding printed cards, natural light. Color-grade is warm/sand, slight desaturation.
- The portrait of Cindy uses a **halftone duotone** in Glycine · Terre · Paille (Terre-surface variant) or Brume · Brume-900 · Nuit (Nuit-surface variant). This is a signature treatment.

### Cards
- **Mostly parchment-on-bone or Paille-on-Terre.**
- Corners are **near-square** (`radius-0` to `radius-2`). Editorial, not pillowy. Buttons can use up to `radius-3`.
- Shadow is minimal — `--shadow-2` for hover lift, nothing on rest.
- A common pattern: a small `01` / `02` set vertically in the gutter, paired with the word `profil` rotated 90°. This is a signature editorial flourish.

### Borders
- Hairlines in `--bone-d` on bone, `--line-on-terre` on Terre, `--line-on-nuit` on Nuit.
- On the Nuit manifesto strip, content is enclosed in a 1px Brume-tinted hairline rectangle with a small uppercase eyebrow label sitting on the top border.

### Buttons

Three primary styles, one per surface:

| Where | Fill | Text | Hover |
|---|---|---|---|
| On **bone**  | Glycine `#E4D1FE` | Terre `#412F21`   | Glycine-700, Paille text, `translateY(-1px)` |
| On **Terre** | Paille `#F2E5C2`  | Terre `#412F21`   | Glycine fill (same direction) |
| On **Nuit**  | Brume `#BAD1FD`   | Nuit `#051833`    | Brume-700, Nuit text |

Secondary / ghost = text + bottom border + trailing `→`, on whatever the local foreground is. Padding `12–14px / 22–26px`, radius `8px`, label UPPERCASE Inter Tight tracked 0.10em.

### Animation
- All transitions ~`240ms` with `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (out-soft) for ease.
- No carousel auto-rotation. No parallax. No scroll-jacking.
- Page-entry rise animations were intentionally removed because they fought DOM-re-render screenshot capture.

### Transparency & blur
- Almost none. The brand is paper-tactile, not glassy. One acceptable use: the contact modal scrim uses `rgba(5, 24, 51, 0.7)` with a `blur(6px)` over the page.

### Layout signatures
- Editorial grid with **wide gutters** and content max-width around `1140–1240px`.
- The vertical "01 / profil" gutter motif (number + rotated word) appears next to project cards.
- Section eyebrows are **centered** in the header band; section bodies are **left-aligned**.
- Value-prop cards are **right-aligned** as a vertical stack next to a left-aligned headline.

### Imagery direction
Warm, golden-hour, slightly desaturated. Hands, paper, printed cards, laptops on linen. The Cindy portrait gets a **Glycine halftone** on Terre, or a **Brume halftone** on Nuit.

## Iconography

### What we observed
The source homepage uses **very few icons**. A small chevron arrow (`→`) sits next to secondary CTAs, and that is essentially it. There's no visible icon font, no Lucide / Feather / Heroicons signature, and no decorative SVG illustrations.

### What we use
- **CDN: Lucide** at stroke width 1.5 — the closest match for the editorial, thin, sober line-icon language.
  - `<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://unpkg.com/lucide-static@latest/font/lucide.css" />` OR import individual SVGs from `https://unpkg.com/lucide-static@latest/icons/<name>.svg`.
  - ⚠️ **Flagged substitution.** If the brand actually uses a different system (or none), this should be replaced.
- **Arrow** `→` (the Unicode RIGHTWARDS ARROW, U+2192) is used inline next to secondary CTAs (`voir mes réalisations →`). This is the only icon-like element that's confirmed-from-source.
- **No emoji.** Never.
- **No decorative SVG illustrations** unless we get explicit assets. Halftone photographic treatments substitute for illustration.

### Sizing
Icons inline with text follow the cap-height of the surrounding type. Standalone icons sit at 20px (small) / 24px (default) / 32px (large feature). Stroke is always 1.5px regardless of size — the editorial register is "fine lines."

### What we explicitly avoid
- ❌ Filled / chunky icon sets (Material Filled, Bootstrap Icons).
- ❌ Two-tone icons.
- ❌ Emoji ANYWHERE — including in placeholders.
- ❌ Hand-rolled SVG illustrations attempting to "represent" a feature.
